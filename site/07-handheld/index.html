Library dex

Ten entries. Read them in order.

  1. 001

    One listSTORES

    Games you own on Steam, GOG and Epic, in one library.

  2. 002

    One game, one rowSTORES

    The same game bought on two stores is one record.

  3. 003

    Your statusLOCAL

    Set your own backlog status for each game.

  4. 004

    Two viewsLOCAL

    A cover wall or a table. Both show the same filter, sort and selection.

  5. 005

    TodayLOCAL

    A screen that proposes what to play next.

  6. 006

    Your wishesOPTIONAL

    Wishlist prices from IsThereAnyDeal. You can also add wishes by hand, one for each device.

  7. 007

    Light, dark or systemLOCAL

    Pick the theme in Utilities.

  8. 008

    Take it outLOCAL

    Export your data at any time.

  9. 009

    DisconnectSTORES

    Disconnect a store. Your notes and your status stay.

  10. 010

    Not a launcherLOCAL

    It never downloads or launches a game. It is a library.

Items

Optional extras, each one a bag item. The app works without them. Without IGDB, titles come from the store and duplicates are still removed.

  • IGDB OPTIONAL

    Covers and metadata. Also time to beat: main story, main + extras, completionist, as players report it to IGDB.

  • IsThereAnyDeal OPTIONAL

    Wishlist prices with a free ITAD key and your country. Current price, the store that sells it, the historical low, sorted by discount.

  • Amazon Luna NO KEY

    No credential needed. A wish that is in the Prime Luna catalogue is marked On Luna. Play it today without buying.

Link cable not required

  • NO SERVER. NO ACCOUNTS.Your library is one SQLite file on your computer.
  • NO CREDENTIAL LEAVES.Credentials go in the system keyring, or in an encrypted file when there is no keyring.
  • NO STORE PASSWORD.The app never asks for one. You log in to GOG or Epic on their own login page.
  • WHY A DESKTOP APP.GOG and Epic logins can only be kept safe on your own machine. A server that keeps the tokens would break their terms, become a target, and one IP ban would stop everyone. Playnite, Heroic and Lutris are desktop apps for the same reason.
