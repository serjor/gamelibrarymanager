Export

Take your data out of the app and keep the file. Your library is yours.

Disconnect a store

Disconnecting marks the store's account and its copies as gone, and removes its credential. Your records, links and your own notes and status stay.

Local first

No server. No accounts. No store credential leaves your computer. The app never asks for a store password: you log in on the store's own page.

Why a desktop app

Sign-in for GOG and Epic can only be kept safe on your own machine. A server would hold your tokens, and one IP ban would stop everyone. Playnite, Heroic and Lutris are desktop apps for the same reason.

Library, not launcher

It never downloads or launches games.
