Rulebook

Six rules, as the game plays. They are the same rules the app follows.

  1. A library, not a launcher.

    It lists your games. It never downloads or launches them.

  2. Local first.

    All data lives in one SQLite file on your machine. There is no server and no account.

  3. No credential leaves your computer.

    Credentials live in the system keyring, or in an encrypted file if there is no keyring. The app never asks for a store password. You sign in on each store's own page.

  4. Each store has its own switch.

    Steam uses its official API with your own key. GOG and Epic have no public API, and Epic uses a private one that can break. You switch a store off. Its games stay in your library, with your notes and status.

  5. Why a desktop app.

    GOG and Epic sign-in can only be defended on your own machine. A server that keeps tokens would break their terms, become a target, and one IP ban would stop everyone. Playnite, Heroic and Lutris are desktop apps for the same reason.

  6. Your data goes with you.

    Export your data at any time. Disconnect a store and keep what you wrote. Choose the Light, Dark or System theme.
