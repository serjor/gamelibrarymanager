Review · Desktop app

Game Library
Manager

A library, not a launcher. It never downloads or starts a game.

Games bought on Steam, GOG and Epic end up in three different lists. Game Library Manager puts them in one library, on your own computer.

Same game, two stores, one record.

Duplicates, gone

The same game bought on two stores is one record, not two. The app removes the copies, and each game keeps one place in your list. You add your own status to each game, so your backlog is in the same place as your games.

Two ways to look

Use the cover wall when you want to see the shelf. Use the table when you want to sort and filter.

Today

The Today screen proposes what to play next. You can choose the Light, Dark or System theme.

Your data

All data lives in one SQLite file on your machine. There is no server and no account. Export your data whenever you want. If you stop using a store, disconnect it: the app keeps your records, your links and what you wrote.

Three stores, three switches

Steam

Uses the official Steam API. You give it your own API key.

GOG

GOG has no public API. You log in on GOG's own login page. The app never asks for your password.

Epic

Epic has no public API. The app uses the private API of the Epic launcher, so it can break any day.

Epic breaks? Flip its switch.

Each store has its own switch. When one breaks, you switch it off. The other two keep running, and the games from the off store stay in your library, with your notes and status. Only you decide to switch a store off.

Local first, on purpose

Store credentials go in the system keyring. If your system has no keyring, they go in an encrypted file. No store credential leaves your computer. The app runs on your computer for a simple reason: GOG and Epic logins can only be kept safe on your own machine. A server that keeps the tokens would break their terms, become a target for attacks, and one IP ban would stop everyone. Playnite, Heroic and Lutris are desktop apps for the same reason.

Tips & Tricks

  1. Epic breaks? Flip its switch. Steam and GOG keep running, and your Epic games stay in the library with your notes.
  2. Linux on Wayland The app turns on WEBKIT_DISABLE_DMABUF_RENDERER=1 for you. To try the renderer, start it with WEBKIT_DISABLE_DMABUF_RENDERER=0.
  3. Linux AppImage won't start? Make it executable first: chmod +x Game.Library.Manager_*_amd64.AppImage

IGDB

Time to beat

Metadata and covers from IGDB. For each game you can see the time to beat: main story, main plus extras, and completionist. The times are what players report to IGDB.
