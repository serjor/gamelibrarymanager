The same game on two stores is one record, not two. You are welcome.

WHAT IT IS

Game Library Manager puts the games you own in Steam, GOG and Epic into one library. Add your own backlog status to each game.

It is a library, not a launcher. It never downloads or launches a game.

PARTY

Three stores. Each one has its own switch.

  • S

    STEAM

    OFFICIAL API
    HP

    Healthy. Uses the official Steam API with your own API key.

    Out of party. Its games stay in your library, with your notes and status.

  • G

    GOG

    NO PUBLIC API
    HP

    Healthy. GOG has no public API. You log in on GOG's own page.

    Out of party. Its games stay in your library, with your notes and status.

  • E

    EPIC

    FRAGILE
    HP

    Uses the private API of its launcher. It can break any day. If it does, switch it off.

    Out of party. Its games stay in your library, with your notes and status.

Each store has its own switch. A switched-off store keeps its games, your notes and your status. The switches on this page only change this picture. They do not touch your library.

ITEMS

Optional extras. The library works without any of them.

IGDB Tome of LoreCovers and metadata from IGDB. Also time to beat: main story, main + extras, and completionist, as players report it to IGDB. Without IGDB, everything still works with the titles from the store, including the removal of duplicates.

ITAD Merchant's LedgerWishlist prices from IsThereAnyDeal: the current price, the store that sells it, the lowest price on record, and the list sorted by discount. Needs a free ITAD key and your country.

Luna ScrollAmazon Luna needs no credential. A wish that is in the Prime Luna catalogue is marked On Luna: play it today without buying it.

Hover or select an item to read what it does.

SKILLS

  • TODAYProposes what to play next.
  • WALL / TABLETwo view modes for the library: a cover wall or a table.
  • THEMELight, Dark or System.
  • WISHESAdd a wish by hand. One for each device, on the same game record as the store copies.
  • EXPORTTake your data out, any time.
  • DISCONNECTDisconnect a store. Your records, links and notes stay.

STATUS

WHERE YOUR DATA LIVES
One SQLite file on your machine.
ACCOUNTS
None. No server. No sign-up.
STORE CREDENTIALS
Stay on your computer. Kept in the system keyring, or in an encrypted file if there is no keyring. No store credential leaves your computer.
STORE PASSWORDS
Never asked. You log in to GOG or Epic on their own login page.

Why a desktop app? GOG and Epic logins can only be guarded on your own machine. A server that keeps their tokens would break their terms, become an attack target, and one IP ban would stop everyone. Playnite, Heroic and Lutris are desktop apps for the same reason.
