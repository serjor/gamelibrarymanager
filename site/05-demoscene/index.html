GAME LIBRARY MANAGER V0.10.0. STEAM + GOG + EPIC IN ONE LIBRARY. NO SERVER, NO ACCOUNTS. YOUR DATA STAYS ON YOUR MACHINE. ONE SWITCH PER STORE. IGDB, ITAD AND LUNA ARE OPTIONAL. NEVER DOWNLOADS, NEVER LAUNCHES A GAME. GREETINGS TO PLAYNITE, HEROIC AND LUTRIS. GPL-3.0-OR-LATER.

The games you own on Steam, GOG and Epic, in one library. Your data stays in one file on your machine. No server, no accounts.

╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║ ► All your data lives in one SQLite file on your machine.    ║
║ ► No server. No accounts.                                    ║
║ ► No store credential leaves your computer.                  ║
║ ► Credentials go to the system keyring. With no keyring, to  ║
║   an encrypted file.                                         ║
║ ► The app never asks for a store password. You log in on the ║
║   store's own page.                                          ║
║                                                              ║
║ Why desktop? GOG and Epic logins can only be defended on     ║
║ your own machine. A server that keeps their tokens would     ║
║ break their terms, become an attack target, and one IP ban   ║
║ would stop everyone. Playnite, Heroic and Lutris are desktop ║
║ apps for the same reason.                                    ║
