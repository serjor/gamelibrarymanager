GAME LIBRARYMANAGER

One library for the games you own in Steam, GOG and Epic. A desktop app for Windows, Linux and macOS. Your data stays on your computer.

STAGE 03

MEMORY CARD

Your library is saved on your computer. Nothing goes to a server.

  • All data lives in one SQLite file on your machine.
  • No store credential leaves your computer. Credentials sit in the system keyring, or in an encrypted file if there is no keyring.
  • The app never asks for a store password. You log in to GOG and Epic on their own login pages.
  • You can export your data. You can disconnect a store and keep what you wrote.

Why a desktop app? GOG and Epic logins can only be defended on your own machine. A server that kept tokens would break their terms, would be an attack target, and one IP ban would stop everyone. Playnite, Heroic and Lutris are desktop apps for the same reason.

STAGE 04

POWER-UPS

Optional. Each power-up adds a feature. Without them, everything still works with the titles from the store, including duplicate removal.

  • IGDB OPTIONAL

    Covers and metadata from IGDB. Also time to beat: main story, main + extras, or completionist, as players report it to IGDB.

  • ITAD OPTIONAL

    Wishlist prices from IsThereAnyDeal. Needs a free ITAD key and your country. Shows the current price, the store that sells it, and the lowest price so far. The list sorts by discount.

    You can also add wishes by hand, for each device.

  • LUNA OPTIONAL

    Amazon Luna. No credential needed. A wish that is in the Prime Luna catalogue is marked On Luna. You can play it today, without buying.
