Setup, in order. Open a split to read its segment.

  1. No. Split Needs Delta
  2. 01 Install FREE Nothing but a download −AHEAD

    Get the file for your system. Run it. That is the install.

    • Windows: the setup .exe (there is also a .msi).
    • Linux: the .AppImage. Make it executable, then run it. A .deb and .rpm are there too.
    • macOS: the .dmg. It is the Apple silicon (aarch64) build.

    Building from source: bun install, then bun run tauri dev.

  3. 02 Connect Steam FREE Your own Steam API key −AHEAD

    Steam is the only one of the three with an official API.

    The app reads your owned games through Steam's API, with your own key. The key goes to your system keyring. If there is no keyring, it goes to an encrypted file on your machine.

  4. 03 Connect GOG FREE You log in on GOG's own page +BEHIND

    GOG has no public API. This split can break.

    The app never asks for your GOG password. You log in on GOG's login page. If the connector breaks, switch GOG off. Your GOG games stay in the library, with your notes and status.

  5. 04 Connect Epic FREE You log in on Epic's own page +BEHIND

    Epic is RNG. If it breaks, skip the split.

    Epic has no public API. The app uses the private API of the Epic launcher. It can change or stop any day. When it does, switch Epic off and carry on. Your Epic games stay, with your notes and status.

    As with GOG, you log in on Epic's page. The app never sees your Epic password.

  6. 05 Dedupe FREE Nothing. It runs on your machine. −AHEAD

    The same game on two stores is one record.

    Duplicates are found across Steam, GOG and Epic. The app matches the titles that each store gives. You do not need IGDB for this.

  7. 06 Set backlog FREE Your own status for each game −AHEAD

    Your status and notes live with the game, not with the store.

    Set a backlog status on each game. Your notes stay when you disconnect a store.

    The Today screen proposes what to play next. The library shows as a cover wall or as a table. Theme is Light, Dark or System.

  8. 07 IGDB OPTIONAL Covers, metadata, time to beat ±NEUTRAL

    Optional. Without it, everything still works.

    IGDB adds covers and metadata. It also gives time to beat: main story, main plus extras, and completionist. These are what players report to IGDB, not your own times.

    Without IGDB, the titles from the store are used, dedupe included.

  9. 08 ITAD prices OPTIONAL Free IsThereAnyDeal key, your country ±NEUTRAL

    Wishlist prices, sorted by discount.

    For each wish: the current price, the store that sells it, and the lowest price on record. The list sorts by discount.

    You can also add wishes by hand, one for each device. A wish for a console gets no price.

  10. 09 Luna Amazon Prime. No credential. ±NEUTRAL

    A wish that is in Luna's Prime catalogue says "On Luna".

    The app reads the Amazon Luna catalogue that Prime includes. No login to Amazon is needed. If the game is in the catalogue, the wish or game record says "On Luna".

    If you have Prime, you can play it today without buying it.

Notes on the route

Route notes

Why the route is built this way.

Note 01 · the RNG split

Epic is RNG. If it breaks, skip the split.

Epic has no public API. The app uses the launcher's private one, and that can change on any day. Nothing you wrote is lost when it does. Switch Epic off, and your games and notes stay where they are.

Note 02 · one switch per store

Each store has its own switch.

A broken store is switched off on its own. The other stores carry on. Its games stay in the library, with your notes and status. Only you decide to switch a store off. The app does not do it for you.

Note 03 · why desktop

Desktop, not web.

GOG and Epic logins can only be defended on your own machine. A server that keeps their tokens breaks their terms, becomes an attack target, and one IP ban would stop everyone at once. Playnite, Heroic and Lutris are desktop apps for the same reason.

Note 04 · what is not on the route

It never launches a game.

It does not download games. It does not start them. It is a library, so the launcher you already use still starts the game. Your data can be exported at any time.
