I

Items

What you carry into the library. Select an item to read it.

Steam Key

Given by the one store that speaks openly. With it, the library reads what you own.

In plain termsSteam's official API, with your own Steam API key.

GOG Ring

This store keeps no public door. You sign in at its own gate, and what it leaves behind stays in your keyring.

In plain termsGOG has no public API. You log in on GOG's own page. The app never asks for your password.

Epic Sigil

Borrowed from the launcher's own tongue. It may fall silent any day. When it does, extinguish it; what it brought remains.

In plain termsEpic has no public API. This uses its launcher's private API, so it can break at any time. Switch it off and your games and notes stay.

Tome of IGDB

Notes kept by scholars of the realm: covers, metadata, and how long each journey takes.

In plain termsOptional. Covers, metadata and time to beat, as players report it to IGDB. Without it, titles come from the store and everything still works.

Lantern of ITAD

A light for the wishes. It shows what they cost now, where, and how low they have gone.

In plain termsOptional. IsThereAnyDeal prices for your wishlist: current price, the store, the historical low. Needs a free ITAD key and your country.

Charm of Luna

A cloud of play, held in a small charm. The Prime catalogue carries it. No key is asked.

In plain termsOptional. Wishes that are in the Amazon Luna catalogue that Prime includes are marked On Luna. No credential needed.

Wish Coin

Cast by your own hand. One for each device. A console gets no price.

In plain termsAdd wishes by hand, one for each device, on the same record as the store copies.

Scroll of Export

Your records, written out so you can carry them away.

In plain termsExport your data. Disconnect a store and the records, links and notes you wrote stay.

II

Messages

Notes left on the ground by those who came before.

  • Try local-first

    Your library is one SQLite file on your machine. Nothing needs a server to work.

  • Be wary of servers

    The app runs no server. A server holding store tokens would break the stores' terms and invite attack.

  • No password ahead

    The app never asks for a store password. You sign in on the store's own login page.

  • Duplicates ahead, therefore one record

    The same game bought on two stores is shown as one record.

III

Bonfire

Rest here. Your progress is kept on your machine.

All your data lives in one SQLite file on your machine. No server. No accounts.

Data

Accounts
None.
Credentials

IV

Estus

Optional blessings. The library works without any of them.

  • IGDB Optional

    Covers, metadata and time to beat: main story, main plus extras, or completionist, as players report it.

  • IsThereAnyDeal Optional

    Wishlist prices, sorted by discount: the current price, the store that sells it, and the historical low. Needs a free ITAD key and your country.

  • Amazon Luna Optional

    Wishes and games in the Prime Luna catalogue are marked On Luna. No key and no credential.

V

Why this path

A desktop app, not a website.

Store sign-in can only be defended on your own machine. A server that keeps tokens would break store terms, become an attack target, and one IP ban would stop everyone. Playnite, Heroic and Lutris are desktop apps too.

The app never downloads or launches games. It is a library, not a launcher.

  • Considered and refused
  • Use a server
  • Accounts
  • A hosted copy
